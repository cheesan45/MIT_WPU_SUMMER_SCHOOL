About Me

Hi, I'm Ahmed! I'm a 9th-grade student at MITCON International School (MIS). I have a deep curiosity for how the world works, which fuels my passion for Science and History. I don't have any pets (allergies in the family!), but I bring plenty of energy and enthusiasm to everything I do. I'm always looking to learn, grow, and eventually make my way to a great college.
